# Incident Report INC-2026-014 — Reefer logger gap

## Summary
On 2026-03-12, shipment SH-8891 (chilled yogurt, Hamburg DC to Berlin store) had a 22-minute logger gap after a traffic delay at the A10.

## What happened
Driver followed SOP-001 delay procedure and notified QC. Temperature stayed at 5C. QC released the load for delivery.

## Follow-up
Install spare logger in all Hamburg reefers. This report does not change the 30-minute delay rule in SOP-001.
