# Shipment Policy — Hazardous goods labeling (POL-HAZ-005)

## Scope
Applies to ADR-classified outbound pallets.

## Labeling
Each pallet must show the UN number and class diamond on two adjacent sides before staging at the dock.

## Driver check
Drivers must photograph labels before departure. Missing diamonds: do not load.

## Out of scope
This policy does not describe customs brokerage, air freight IATA packing, or customer freight rates.
