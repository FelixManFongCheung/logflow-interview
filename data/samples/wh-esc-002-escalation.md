# Warehouse Escalation Procedure (WH-ESC-002)

## When to escalate
Escalate to the shift lead when:
- A pick face is empty for an SKU on an active wave for more than 20 minutes.
- A dock door is blocked and the next appointment is within 45 minutes.
- A safety incident (near miss or injury) occurs.

## How to escalate
1. Page the shift lead with location, wave id, and impact.
2. If no response in 10 minutes, page the warehouse manager.
3. Safety incidents skip the wait: call emergency services first, then notify the manager.

## Do not
Do not reassign dock appointments without the Docken coordinator.
