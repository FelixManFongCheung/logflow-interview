# Customer Handling Notes — ACME Retail (CUST-ACME-003)

## Delivery windows
ACME stores accept inbound only 06:00-11:00 local. Late arrivals after 11:00 must be rebooked; do not wait on site.

## Special handling
- No tail-lift required at stores with dock levelers (codes A1-A4).
- Pallet exchange: ACME requires CHEP pallets only.
- POD must include the store stamp. Photo POD is not accepted.

## Contacts
Store inbound desk is listed in the TMS customer master. This document does not include freight rates.
