# Cold Chain SOP (SOP-001)

## Scope
Applies to all temperature-controlled (reefer) outbound deliveries for tenant warehouses.

## Delay procedure
If a cold-chain delivery is delayed more than 30 minutes past the booked slot:
1. Notify the Quality Control (QC) on-call within 10 minutes.
2. Record the current trailer temperature from the logger.
3. If temperature has exceeded the allowed band for more than 15 minutes, quarantine the load and do not deliver.
4. If temperature is still in band, proceed and add a delay note on the proof of delivery.

## Allowed temperature band
Chilled: 2C to 8C. Frozen: -25C to -18C.

## Escalation
QC on-call: warehouse radio channel QC-1. After 21:00 local, call the night supervisor.
